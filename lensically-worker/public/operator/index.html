Questions before ownership

Everything that should be clear before you buy.

Is this a managed service?

No. Lensically Operator is a customer-owned software package and commercial license. The installation workflow is AI-led, but the repository, infrastructure, platform accounts, and operational data belong to the buyer.

What exactly happens after payment?

Stripe redirects you to a Lensically verification page. After the paid Checkout Session is verified, you receive a short-lived one-time download for the private v1.0.0 ZIP, its SHA-256 checksum, and a recorded license key.

Do I need to be a developer?

You need to be comfortable creating and approving customer-owned accounts, signing in, completing 2FA, authorizing OAuth, entering protected secrets, and accepting third-party terms. The included installation authority is designed for ChatGPT or Codex to perform the routine technical work.

Which third-party accounts are required?

A customer-owned GitHub account or organization, Cloudflare account, Meta Developer account, Threads account, and AI access capable of running the installation and connected Operator workflow. A custom domain is optional.

Are Cloudflare, GitHub, Meta, or AI usage costs included?

No. The purchase price covers the Lensically software license. Third-party services use the buyer’s own accounts and remain governed by their pricing, terms, availability, and approval requirements.

Can an agency deploy it for clients?

Yes, when customer-specific deployments remain owned and controlled by the purchaser. The source package itself cannot be redistributed, sublicensed, resold, published, or shared, and a substantially equivalent white-label package cannot be sold.

Does Lensically guarantee followers, reach, or revenue?

No. Lensically provides operational infrastructure, workflows, evidence, controls, continuity, and recovery. Audience growth, reach, revenue, continuous third-party availability, and platform approval are not guaranteed.

What support and refund protection are included?

v1.x product-defect fixes are included. The refund policy covers a verified product defect reported within the stated window when it prevents the documented installation and cannot be repaired after reasonable cooperation.
