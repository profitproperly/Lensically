Questions before ownership

Still deciding?

Is this a managed service?

No. Lensically Operator is a customer-owned software package and commercial license. The installation workflow is AI-led, but the repository and infrastructure belong to the buyer.

Does Lensically guarantee followers, reach, revenue, or platform approval?

No. The system provides operational infrastructure, workflows, evidence, continuity, and controls. Audience growth, revenue, continuous availability, and third-party approval are not guaranteed.

Can an agency use it for customer-specific deployments?

Yes, when those deployments remain owned and controlled by the purchaser. The source package itself cannot be redistributed, sublicensed, resold, published, or shared, and a substantially equivalent white-label package cannot be sold.

Are Cloudflare, Meta, GitHub, or OpenAI costs included?

No. Third-party services use the buyer’s own accounts and are governed by their own pricing, limits, availability, and terms.

What happens after payment?

Stripe redirects to a Lensically verification page. After the paid session is verified, the buyer receives a short-lived one-time download for the private v1.0.0 release, its SHA-256 checksum, and a recorded license key.

What support is included?

v1.x product-defect fixes are included. The refund policy covers a verified product defect reported within the stated window when it prevents the documented installation and cannot be repaired after reasonable cooperation.

What accounts do I need?

A GitHub account, a Cloudflare account, a Meta Developer account and Threads authorization, plus ChatGPT, Codex, or compatible OpenAI access. A custom domain is optional.

Can I inspect and modify the code?

Yes. The commercial license permits the purchaser to use and modify the software for their own business and controlled customer-specific deployments, subject to the license restrictions.
